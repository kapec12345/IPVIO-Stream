# K-011 – Compact live TV on Kino

Entering live TV with small or normal rows opens All channels. Channel names and the current program share a compact row, with a small logo, persistent focus outline and program details at the right. The live landing page's alternative genre shelves also use compact cards. The large row option keeps the larger presentation. The channel menu, quality selection, favorites, categories, guide and search remain accessible.

The Philips displayed ten complete channel rows at once at its normal row setting. Remote Right entered the channel list and Down moved selection from TVN to TVN 7, updating the current title and upcoming programs in the detail pane.
