# K-006 – Movie and series posters in Kino

Replace cropped episode stills with title-specific poster artwork when available. Center the best available image at native size or smaller, preserving the whole picture. KinoPoster handles this for VOD poster tiles, search and poster fallbacks. The existing image loader and caches handle requests. Small TMDB variants can be upgraded, with the original URL available if the larger version fails. Series use the provider series cover in preference to the playlist episode image. Movie cards can use their existing full-size playlist posters. Provider lookups are delayed and limited to two simultaneous requests. ProviderInfo serializes refreshes of its shared series catalogue.

The 1.4.6-pl (11) preview APK passed archive and signature checks. Sixteen focused tests passed across KinoPosterTest, ProviderLogicTest and SavedStylesTest. The update was installed over 1.4.5 on the Philips. The physical TV series grid showed full portrait artwork and an uncropped horizontal image for Big Sky. Cache checks confirmed that Melania Rea, VisionQuest and Balaraw had 600 × 900 series covers available in place of their 185 × 104 playlist stills. The film posters for Ślepa furia and Znak smoka measured 600 × 900. The viewer confirmed the improvement on the TV.
